## Brand & Style

This design system drives a cutting-edge, conversion-focused educational platform tailored for engineers, data practitioners, and tech leaders advancing into Agentic AI, GenAI, and Data Science.

### Brand Personality & Archetype
- **The Architect of Tomorrow:** Hyper-competent, precise, innovative, and deeply pragmatic.
- **Tone:** Technical yet accessible, inspiring, authoritative, and direct. The interface avoids frivolous ornamentation in favor of crisp data structures, transparent curriculum roadmaps, and immediate career outcomes.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence, momentum, high value perception, and technological empowerment. Learners should feel they are entering a premier technical laboratory, not a generic online classroom.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Technical Precision with Subtle Glassmorphism:** Deep navy and slate canvas foundations punctuated by radiant electric cyan/blue accents and vivid emerald validation markers.
- **Structural Conversion Focus:** High-density, high-legibility layout patterns that clearly communicate syllabi, dynamic code previews, active student batches, and verified placement outcomes through tactile card hierarchies and luminous pill markers.

## Colors

The system employs a dark-first foundation to mirror terminal/IDE environments, with surgical bursts of luminescence indicating progression, interactivity, and successful validation.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#0B1120`):** Ultra-deep slate-navy. Establishes the deepest horizon.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0F172A`):** Core container background for primary cards, sidebars, and course modules.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#1E293B`):** Elevated inner surfaces, code blocks, active tab states, and nested card groupings.
- **Surface Glass Overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`): Translucent backdrop with a `16px` blur for fixed navigation, modals, and sticky conversion bars.
- **Borders & Dividers:** High-precision, low-friction outlines using `#334155` at default, transitioning to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)` on interactive focus.

### Accent & Feedback Palette
- **Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Primary interactive focal point, terminal prompts, and active course tracks.
- **Hyper Blue (`#2563EB` / `#3B82F6`):** Secondary accent for system progress bars, primary button gradients, and structural links.
- **Verified Emerald (`#10B981` / `#059669`):** Reserved strictly for high-conversion signals: career placement statistics, live cohort indicators, project passing states, and credential validations.
- **High-Contrast Text:** 
  - Primary Headlines: `#F8FAFC`
  - Secondary/Body: `#94A3B8`
  - Subtle/Meta: `#64748B`

## Typography

The typography pairings bridge technical rigor and high-converting marketing impact.

### Type Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric, modern, and distinct. Its deliberate curve apexes and tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) deliver high authority on dark backgrounds.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse engineered for complex UI layouts, syllabi lists, and multi-paragraph technical explanations.
- **Data & System Tags (JetBrains Mono):** Injected intentionally across cohort tags, live status counters, code syntax snippets, and curriculum metrics to anchor the institute's technical credibility.

### Usage Standards
- Always render `display-hero` with an optional two-word gradient clip matching the primary-to-secondary scale (`#06B6D4` to `#3B82F6`) for pivotal headlines.
- Maintain a minimum of `1.5x` line-height on all body copy to prevent visual fatigue on dark canvas backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is constructed on a 12-column responsive grid system optimized for content density, fast visual parsing, and high conversion flow.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, `1280px` maximum content width container, `gutter` of `1.5rem`, outer canvas `margin` of `3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid, `gutter` of `1.25rem`, outer canvas `margin` of `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`, outer canvas `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem`.

### Spacing Cadence
- Internal card padding defaults strictly to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on desktop and steps down to `space-md` (`1rem`) on mobile.
- Section vertical spacing follows an alternating cadence: `5rem` between related thematic modules, `7rem` between major transition points (e.g., from Curriculum Track to Placement Outcomes).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal surfaces, micro-glows, and frosted translucent barriers rather than heavy, dirty drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0B1120`. Non-elevated foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Card Default):** `#0F172A` accompanied by a `1px` structural outline (`#1E293B`).
- **Layer 2 (Elevated / Hover):** `#1E293B` resting above `#0F172A`. Outlined with a cyan micro-glow border (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`).
- **Layer 3 (Floating Modals & Sticky CTA):** Translucent backdrop using `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ambient downward diffusion: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.8)`.

### Technical Glow Shadows
- **Action Pulse:** `0 0 20px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)` applied strictly to primary interactive elements and high-priority cohort registration buttons.
- **Success Glow:** `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` applied to active enrollment badges and placement validation chips.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp, technical discipline with modern, approachable ergonomics.

### Curvature Taxonomy
- **Containers & Major Cards (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`):** Applied to syllabus containers, pricing matrices, and review cards.
- **Interactive Elements & Fields (`rounded` / `0.5rem`):** Buttons, code snippet containers, and form inputs utilize `0.5rem` (`8px`) to maintain structural precision.
- **Badges, Tags, and Chips (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Status flags, cohort indicators, and prerequisite tags leverage pure pill geometry to contrast against rectilinear card structures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion):** Linear gradient from `#06B6D4` to `#2563EB`, text color `#0B1120` or `#FFFFFF` (bold `label-ui`), `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Resting state features subtle cyan ambient elevation. On hover, transform `translateY(-1px)` and elevate glow opacity.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base, `1px` border in `#334155`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover, background shifts to `#1E293B` and border changes to `#06B6D4`.
- **Outcome / Direct Apply (Success):** Solid `#10B981`, text `#0B1120`, font weight `700`. Used exclusively for "Enroll Now", "Claim Scholarship", or "Accept Offer" states.

### Premium Pill Badges
- **Active Cohort / Live:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, text `#10B981`, typography `label-mono-sm`. Accompanied by a `6px` pulsing emerald circle marker.
- **Specialization Tag (GenAI / Agentic AI):** Background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`, text `#06B6D4`, typography `label-mono-sm`.

### Conversion-Oriented Cards
- **Curriculum & Track Cards:** Background `#0F172A`, `1px` border `#1E293B`, `1.5rem` internal padding. Top header contains the course icon, title, and floating JetBrains Mono duration badge. Bottom area provides modular stats (projects, toolstack, hiring partners) split by hairline `#1E293B` horizontal rules.
- **Placement & Salary Proof Cards:** Glass-tinted surface, `#10B981` metric highlights for average compensation hike, student previous vs. current role comparison tag, and verified credential link.

### Form Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#0B1120`, border `1px solid #334155`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`, typography `body-md`, `0.5rem` radius, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions cleanly to `#06B6D4` with an outer ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`.

### Lists & Curriculum Accordions
- **Module Rows:** Enclosed containers with `#0F172A` background. Left edge contains a numeric index using `label-mono-md` in `#06B6D4`.
- **Completed / Active Lessons:** Trailing checkbox rendered with `#10B981` fill and clean white check glyph.
